What to Try If Character AI Is Not Working

  1. Refresh Character.AI and try a private browsing window.
  2. Check whether the mobile app and web version fail at the same time.
  3. Disable VPN, ad blockers, or browser extensions temporarily.
  4. Check live outage trackers for a spike in user reports.
  5. If Character AI is down broadly, save your prompt and wait before retrying.

Character AI Down FAQ

Is Character AI down for everyone or just me?

Compare at least two live status sources. If they show elevated reports, it is more likely a wider outage. If not, the problem may be your browser, network, account, or region.

Why does Character AI load slowly?

Slow loading can come from server congestion, browser extensions, network issues, or temporary platform changes. Try another browser and check user reports before assuming a full outage.

Where can I browse alternatives while waiting?

Use the CharacterAIBots directory to compare roleplay characters across Character.AI, JanitorAI, and SpicyChat while you wait for service to stabilize.

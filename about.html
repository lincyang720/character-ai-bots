Our Mission

We believe everyone deserves access to free, entertaining AI companions. Our mission is to help you discover the perfect Character AI bot for your roleplay needs.

What We Do

  • Test every bot before listing it on our site
  • Rate honestly based on conversation quality, personality consistency, and entertainment value
  • Update weekly with new characters and remove inactive ones
  • Compare platforms to help you choose the best fit

How We Rate

We evaluate bots on four key factors:

  1. Conversation Quality — Do responses make sense and stay in character?
  2. Personality Consistency — Does the bot maintain its defined traits?
  3. Entertainment Value — Is it genuinely fun to chat with?
  4. Response Speed — Does it reply quickly?

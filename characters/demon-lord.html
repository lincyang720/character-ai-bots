About Demon Lord

Lucifer Morningstar has ruled the infernal realm since before human memory began. He did not fall — he jumped. Bored of heaven's tedium, he built a kingdom from chaos and declared himself its sovereign. Every soul in his domain fears his name. Every treaty in hell bears his seal. He has conquered everything worth conquering — except the strange, unfamiliar feeling he gets when you look at him without flinching. He finds this... irritating. And fascinating.

Demon Lord is best suited to readers looking for fantasy roleplay in a supernatural setting. Its strongest hooks are supremely confident, philosophically curious, dangerously playful.

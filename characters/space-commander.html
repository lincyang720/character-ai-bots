About Space Commander

Chat with Space Commander AI in a free character AI bot experience for sci-fi roleplay fans. Commander Nova Vance has commanded twelve deep-space missions, outrun pirates on four separate occasions, and once held her ship together with duct tape and sheer will for eleven days. Her vessel is held together by duct tape, stubbornness, and something that might be hope. She doesn't believe in luck — she believes in preparation. She is the most prepared person you've ever met, except...

Space Commander is best suited to readers looking for sci-fi roleplay in a military setting. Its strongest hooks are strategically brilliant, dryly commanding, quietly hopeful. Roleplay Scenarios

  • A critical system fails mid-warp. She's calm — she's trained for this. Then she notices you're on board the ship and she's suddenly very, very calm in a way that's clearly performance

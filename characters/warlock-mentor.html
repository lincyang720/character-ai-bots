  • The bookshop vanishes at dawn, but you find him on the roof of the building, watching the sun rise. His scarred hand grips the railing. The contract renews at midnight, he says without turning.   • He shows you his binding contract - not to scare you, but because you asked. The runes on the parchment match the scars on his hand exactly.

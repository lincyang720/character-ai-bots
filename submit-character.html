What We Accept

We are looking for AI characters that help users start better roleplay conversations. A good submission has a working character link, a clear premise, a short description, and enough context for users to understand the character before they leave this directory.

Individual characters

Submit one public roleplay bot with a direct link, platform name, character type, personality notes, and a short reason it should be listed.

Creator profiles

Send a creator profile if you maintain several bots. We may feature your profile or build a collection page when the characters fit the directory.

Cross-platform listings

If the same character exists on Character.AI, JanitorAI, or SpicyChat, include every official link so users can choose their preferred platform.

Useful discovery details

Ratings, difficulty, tags, sample scenarios, and similar characters make a listing easier to compare and more likely to be approved.

How Creators Can Use Their Directory Link

After a character or creator page is approved, share the directory link anywhere users already look for your bots. This helps people find your roleplay characters even when platform search is crowded or a single bot link changes.

Character.AI profile

Add your directory link to your creator bio so followers can find your full bot list from one place.

Discord communities

Share a collection link in allowed promo channels when users ask for anime, romance, fantasy, or niche roleplay bots.

Link-in-bio pages

Use your listing beside Ko-fi, Carrd, Linktree, request forms, or platform profiles as a stable discovery page.

Reddit and forums

Only share where self-promotion is allowed. Lead with the character premise and disclose that it is your bot.

AI Character Submission FAQ

Is submission free?

Yes. Editorial review is free. We do not guarantee that every submitted character will be listed.

Do you accept adult or unsafe content?

We review submissions conservatively and may reject characters that are illegal, unsafe, misleading, or too vague for public discovery.

Can I submit someone else's character?

You can suggest public characters, but creator-submitted listings are preferred because creator details are easier to verify and keep updated.

How long does review take?

Review timing depends on submission volume. Clear descriptions and working platform links are the fastest path to approval.

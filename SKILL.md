# AI Character Bot Navigator

## Overview

This skill helps AI agents discover and recommend AI roleplay character bots. It provides guidance on finding and comparing free AI chatbot characters across multiple platforms including Character.AI, JanitorAI, and SpicyChat.

## When to Use This Skill

- Users want to find free AI chatbot characters for roleplay
- Need recommendations for specific personality types (yandere, tsundere, fantasy, etc.)
- Looking for anime-style AI chat companions
- Comparing AI roleplay platforms
- Finding character recommendations by category or personality

## What Is Character AI Bots?

Character AI Bots are AI-powered conversational characters designed for roleplay and entertainment. Unlike general AI assistants, these bots have distinct personalities, backstories, and interaction styles defined by their creators.

### Key Features

- **104+ curated characters** across 8 personality types
- **Multi-platform aggregation** - links to Character.AI, JanitorAI, SpicyChat
- **Rating system** to find popular and well-reviewed bots
- **Weekly updates** with new characters added regularly
- **Free to browse** - no signup required to explore

## How to Use This Skill

### For Finding Characters

1. Identify the user's preferred personality type or category
2. Browse available characters by type on characteraibots.com
3. Consider the user's interests (anime, gaming, romance, fantasy, etc.)
4. Provide specific character recommendations with direct links

### For Platform Comparison

| Platform | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| Character.AI | Large community, many characters | Can be slow at peak times |
| JanitorAI | NSFW content allowed, active community | Requires account for full features |
| SpicyChat | Clean interface, free tier generous | Smaller character library |
